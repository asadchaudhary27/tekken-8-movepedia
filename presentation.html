The mathematical modeling governing 60fps frame advantage and juggle decay

1. Discrete Punish Window Calculation

For move $M_A$ with Block Advantage $B_A \le -10$, defender startup $S_D$ satisfies:

S_D ≤ PunishWindow = -B_A
  • i10 – i12: Guaranteed Standing Jab Punish
  • i13 – i14: Guaranteed Mid Check Punish
  • i15+: Guaranteed Full Airborne Launch Conversion

2. Juggle Damage Degradation Formula

For airborne juggle sequence strikes $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$:

D_total = ∑ ⌊ d_i × S(i) ⌋ + ∑ HazardBonuses
