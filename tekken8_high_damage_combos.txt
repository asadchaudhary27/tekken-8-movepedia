==================================================
HOW TO READ THE NOTATION
==================================================
1 = left punch, 2 = right punch, 3 = left kick, 4 = right kick
1+2 = press together
f = forward, b = back, d = down, u = up, d/f = down-forward, etc.
Capital letter (F, D/F, B) = hold that direction
>  (shown as ►) = next part of the combo
W!  = wall splat          WB! = wall bounce
FBl! = floor blast        BB! = balcony break
WS = while standing       WR = while running (iWR = instant WR)
CC = crouch cancel        FC = full crouch
SSL / SSR = side step left / right
* = special timing (hold max or perfect input), check the note
Heat Burst / Heat Smash / Rage Art need Heat or Rage to be active.
